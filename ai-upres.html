AI Up-Res for Archival and Historical Footage

We use AI to up-res archival and historical footage: low-resolution video, VHS and broadcast tape, Super 8, 8mm and 16mm film transfers, and the 240p clips that now survive only as old uploads online. The result is clean 1080p or 4K footage that holds up next to modern material in a documentary, a brand film or a campaign. Instead of sharpening or interpolating pixels, AI video restoration rebuilds the frame, re-rendering faces, fabric, light and film grain so the shot reads as if it had been filmed on a better camera, while the framing, timing, gestures and lip movement of the original stay intact. It works for archival speeches and news footage, company history, old home movies and any historical footage that deserves a second life on a modern screen. Because the AI recreates detail rather than recovering it, we treat each shot as a remaster, check it against the source, and label it as AI-restored wherever accuracy matters.
